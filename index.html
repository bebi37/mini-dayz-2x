Field Notes / 004

PATCH NOTES.

Follow the latest changes and improvements.

BETA v0.1.44

CAMERA RECOIL, DAY/NIGHT RAIDS & ART FIXES

Added stronger weapon-specific camera recoil and screen shake, with quick recovery, capped automatic-fire movement, and separate saved 0–100% settings. Effects apply only to the local player's camera when shots successfully fire.

Choose day or night before confirming a raid. Fixed Cinematic Mode for raids and the lobby: F8 toggles the HUD and melee cursor correctly, and the accidental three-finger shortcut is removed. Unlocked the melee-cursor option; backstabs deal half damage against enemies that have already detected or targeted you. Fixed false wildlife-detection alerts.

Refined Makarov, extended-magazine Desert Eagle, stripped Assault M249, and RPK extended-magazine sprites and attachments. Restored firetruck collision, removed invisible petrol-station walls, and added the instrumental tracks Plastic Love and Color Your Night to the music selection.

See the full v0.1.44 patch notes.
