### Install

**Windows**
1. Download the ZIP from the table above.
2. Extract the complete ZIP to a folder.
3. Launch the game's executable from the extracted folder; keep the accompanying Unity files together.

**Android**
1. Download the APK matching your device's architecture.
2. Open the APK on your Android device and follow the installation prompts.
3. Android may ask you to allow installation from the app used to open the APK. Only install files you trust.

**Before uninstalling or reinstalling:** Use the game's **save export** feature to back up your progress, then keep the exported backup somewhere safe. You can restore it through **save import**.

## 🛠️ What's new in v0.1.42?

- **Weapon balance:** Updated damage for knives, cleaver, bayonet, shovel, axes, FN FAL, VSS, SKS, SVD and SV-98. Weapon upgrades keep their family damage; backstab and critical-hit mechanics are unchanged.
- **Combat timing:** Bat Knockdown cooldowns reduced by 40%; Shovel Slash cooldown increased by 60%.
- **Auto-raids:** Normal and shore trips now cost **half the usual food** (rounded up). Added an Experimental label; mobile players can hold an unavailable survivor profile for 0.65 seconds to access Fuel speed-up confirmation.
- **Settings backup:** Export/import settings, controls and layouts. Save backup tools remain available from the camp menu.
- **Mobile and UI:** Improved inventory scrolling and swipe controls, enlarged building tap targets, restored original action icons, and changed default aiming settings (auto-aim off). Existing customized settings are preserved.
- **Stealth and world:** Restored tall-grass concealment. Walking sight and footstep detection ranges are 10% shorter; fixed aim geometry and other visibility issues.
- **Loot and fixes:** Doubled car-container loot chances (capped at 100%), improved Chayky Ruins monument placement, restored the factory road, and fixed bulk-ammo crafting and LAN roof visibility.
