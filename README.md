### Install

**Windows**
1. Download the ZIP from the table above.
2. Extract the complete ZIP to a folder.
3. Launch the game's executable from the extracted folder; keep the accompanying Unity files together.

**Android**
1. Download the APK matching your device's architecture.
2. Open the APK on your Android device and follow the installation prompts.
3. Android may ask you to allow installation from the app used to open the APK. Only install files you trust.

**Before uninstalling or reinstalling:** Use the game's **save export** feature to back up your progress, then keep the exported backup somewhere safe. You can restore it through **save import**.

## 📰 Previous update highlights — v0.1.47

- **New GUI:** Updated storage, inventory, ground/container loot and raid-results interfaces, with Classic GUI and reverse-panel-order settings.
- **Combat & audio:** Extraction, death, headshot and precision-preparation sounds; snipe/precision cancellation and shared cooldown fixes; updated bullet trails, muzzle effects and Tagila animations.
- **Auto-raids:** Optional cancellation with equipment preserved, no loot or supply refund, and longer difficulty-based timers.
- **Movement & AI:** Consistent eight-direction movement, fairer stealth detection, NPC noise fixes and improved chest placement.
- **Backpacks:** Rebalanced capacity bonuses and a new Legendary Tactical Backpack (31 kg bonus; 2% per chest on 5-skull+ maps).
- **Loot balance:** Targeted ground and chest loot changes across 16 maps.
- **Benchmark:** Map selector, zoom/pan controls and up to 5,000 simulation runs.
- **Co-op interactions:** Safer item drops, reachable door interactions, better mobile target selection and bandages that stop teammate bleeding.
